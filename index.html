Transparent Workflow

Our 4-Step Studio Pipeline

You approve every major milestone before we move forward—so there are zero surprises at final delivery.

01 Kickoff

Brief & References

Share your concept art, sketches, scale requirements, or brand guidelines. We lock in the timeline, deliverables, and target format.

02 Checkpoint

ZBrush Clay Sculpt Review

We block out anatomy, silhouette, and fine details in ZBrush clay. You inspect turnaround views and request silhouette or pose tweaks.

03 Engineering

Texturing / Rigging / Print-Prep

Depending on your goal: 4K Substance PBR texturing & rigging for games/video, or hollowing, keying, and joint tolerance checks for 3D printing.

04 Launch

Final Production Delivery

Clean, organized production files delivered ready for immediate printing, engine import, or marketing release—plus high-res beauty renders.
